For UK SMBs, automation is key to staying competitive, but choosing between n8n and Zapier often comes down to two factors: **cost** and **control**. Let’s break down the winner for British small-to-medium businesses.

## Pricing
- **Zapier**: Starts at $19.99/month for 750 tasks, but jumps quickly. A typical UK SMB with 5,000 tasks pays £100+/month, with limits on premium apps.
- **n8n**: Open-source self-hosted version is **free**. Cloud plans start at €20/month for 5,000 tasks. For UK businesses, n8n can slash costs by 60-80%, especially at scale.

**Winner: n8n** – especially for businesses with growing task volumes.

## Control
- **Zapier**: Black-box platform. Your data goes through US servers – potential GDPR concerns for UK firms. you can’t customise logic deeply.
- **n8n**: Self-host on UK servers (e.g., AWS London). Full code-level control with custom JavaScript. Use native UK apps like Xero, Sage, and HMRC APIs without restrictions.

**Winner: n8n** – data sovereignty and unlimited flexibility win for compliance-heavy UK SMBs.

## Usability
- **Zapier**: Drag-and-drop simplicity is great for non-technical teams, but limited for complex workflows.
- **n8n**: Visual builder is almost as easy, but adds branching, loops, error handling, and webhook triggers. A learning curve, but pays off.

**Tie** – n8n is more powerful once mastered.

## Integrations
- **Zapier**: 7,000+ apps – vast but increasingly uses premium connectors.
- **n8n**: 400+ native nodes, but you can build custom integrations via HTTP, GraphQL, or any API. For UK-specific tools (e.g., GoCardless, Starling), n8n often has better support.

**Winner: n8n** for UK-specific needs; Zapier for sheer volume.

## Verdict
For UK SMBs prioritising **cost control and data governance**, n8n is the clear winner. Zapier suits quick setups, but long-term n8n offers better customisation, lower bills, and UK compliance.
