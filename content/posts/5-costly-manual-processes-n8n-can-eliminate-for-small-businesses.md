Running a small business means juggling dozens of tasks, many of which are manual, repetitive, and error-prone. Here are five costly manual processes that n8n can automate, freeing your team to focus on growth.

**1. Invoice Processing and Payment Reminders**
Manually chasing payments and creating invoices wastes hours each month. With n8n, you can automatically generate invoices from sales data, send them to customers, and trigger payment reminders after a set number of days. For example, when a payment is due, n8n can send a polite email and update your accounting software like Xero or QuickBooks.

**2. Lead Capture and Qualification**
Hand-entering leads from your website or LinkedIn into a CRM is tedious and prone to typos. n8n can capture form submissions, enrich them with data from public sources, and add them to your CRM (e.g., HubSpot or Salesforce) instantly. You can even score leads based on criteria like industry or budget before notifying your sales team.

**3. Social Media Posting and Monitoring**
Planning and scheduling posts across platforms can be time-consuming. n8n can auto-post content to Twitter, LinkedIn, or Facebook at scheduled times and even track mentions or comments, then alert you or log them to a spreadsheet for follow-up.

**4. Customer Support Ticket Routing**
When support emails come in, manually assigning them to the right person causes delays. n8n can read incoming tickets from tools like Zendesk or a shared inbox, categorise them by keywords (e.g., 'billing' or 'bug'), and assign them to the appropriate team member via Slack or email.

**5. Data Backup and Reporting**
Small businesses often forget to back up critical data or compile weekly reports manually. n8n can copy files from Google Drive to an external server nightly and generate a summary report of key metrics (sales, website traffic, etc.) sent to your inbox every Monday morning.

By automating these five processes, UK SMBs can reduce errors, cut labour costs, and reclaim hours each week. It’s time to stop doing the work a machine can do.
