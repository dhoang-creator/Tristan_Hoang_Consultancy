If you manage rental properties, you know the monthly ritual: send reminders, chase late payers, reconcile accounts, and follow up on missed payments. It’s easy to assume the staff time spent on this is the biggest cost. But look closer, and you’ll see it’s just the tip of the iceberg. 

**1. The Hidden Costs of Manual Rent Chasing**
Manual rent chasing doesn’t just eat hours—it costs you money you might not even realize. Think about late payment fees you waive for regular offenders, the opportunity cost of cash tied up in arrears, and the strain on your team’s morale. A single missed rent chase can cascade into delayed invoices, strained tenant relationships, and even legal fees if you escalate. According to a 2023 survey from the National Residential Landlords Association, nearly 30% of UK landlords report rent delays of more than 14 days at least once a quarter. That’s lost income waiting to be recovered.

**2. The True Impact on Your Business**
For a small portfolio of, say, 20 properties, manual chasing might take 10-15 hours a month. That’s roughly £500–£750 in staff time (at £12/hr). But add in the average late payment of, say, £250 per tenant per year, and you’re looking at £5,000 in lost revenue annually. Suddenly, the staff time seems minor. Plus, the stress of chasing means your team spends less time on growth activities like property maintenance or tenant screening.

**3. How n8n Automation Can Transform Rent Management**
n8n is an open-source automation platform that lets you connect your rent management tools (like accounting software, email, and your tenant database) without coding. Here’s a practical example:

- **Automated reminders**: Set up an n8n workflow that pulls rent due dates from your CRM, checks payment status from your bank’s API (like GoCardless or Stripe), and sends personalized email or SMS reminders to late payers. No manual intervention needed.
