Every UK small business knows the sting of a stock error—a miscount in the warehouse, a supplier mix-up, or a system sync failure. But what happens when those errors never get fixed? They snowball into lost sales, unhappy customers, and hidden revenue drains that hit your bottom line harder than you think.

## The True Cost of Unresolved Stock Errors

Consider this: a single stock error can mean a customer orders an item you think you have, but you don't. You cancel the order, refund the payment, and lose that sale. If it happens 10 times a month with an average order value of £50, that's £6,000 a year gone. Scale that across your entire inventory, and the numbers pile up fast.

Research suggests stock inaccuracies cost retailers up to 10% of their annual revenue in the UK. For a £500k turnover business, that's £50,000 vanishing into thin air—not through poor sales, but because your stock data doesn't match reality.

## Why Stock Errors Persist

Most stock errors are never fixed because manual correction is time-consuming and error-prone. Teams rely on spreadsheets, pen-and-paper counts, or legacy systems that don't talk to each other. When a discrepancy appears, it gets logged, but with daily pressures—serving customers, managing suppliers—fixing it drops down the priority list. Over time, these errors compound.

## The n8n Fix: Automated Stock Reconciliation

This is where n8n automation comes in. n8n is a powerful, open-source tool that connects your existing systems—like Shopify, Xero, or your warehouse management software—and automates workflows without coding.

**Here's how you can stop the leak:**
1. **Real-time syncing:** Connect your sales channels to your inventory database. When a product sells online, n8n automatically updates stock levels everywhere—no delays, no manual entry.
2. **Error detection alerts:** Set up workflows that flag mismatches instantly. For example, if your POS says you have 10 units but your warehouse counts 8, n8n sends a Slack message or email to your team within seconds.
3. **Automated corrections:** Instead of letting errors sit, n8n can trigger corrective actions. For low-stock items, it might reorder from suppliers automatically. For count discrepancies, it can cross-reference audit logs to find the source.
