When a placement falls through, it’s not just an inconvenience—it’s a direct hit to your agency’s bottom line. Slow follow-up is one of the most underestimated culprits, costing UK recruitment firms tens of thousands each year. Let’s break down the numbers.

**The direct cost of a lost placement**

A typical UK recruitment agency charges between 15% and 30% of a candidate’s first-year salary. For a £40,000 placement at 20%, that’s £8,000 in lost revenue. If you lose just one placement per month due to slow follow-up, that’s £96,000 a year. But the real cost goes deeper.

**Hidden costs multiply the damage**

- **Time wasted**: Your recruiters spend hours chasing candidates who have already accepted another offer. That time could have been spent on high-value activities like sourcing or closing. At an average recruiter salary of £35,000 + overheads, each hour costs around £25–£30. A slow follow-up that loses a placement might consume 10–15 hours across the team, adding £300–£450 in wasted labour per incident.
- **Reputation damage**: Slow responses make your agency look unprofessional. Candidates ghost you, clients find another supplier, and your brand erodes. Rebuilding trust with a client after a failed placement can cost 3–5 times more than retaining them.
- **Lost referrals**: Happy candidates and clients refer others. One lost placement can mean 2–3 future referrals vanish, each worth similar placement fees. Over a year, that compounds into a six-figure opportunity cost.

**Why slow follow-up happens**

Most agencies rely on manual email, spreadsheets, and ad-hoc reminders. A candidate expresses interest, you promise to send details, but then a client meeting delays you. By the time you follow up, they’ve accepted elsewhere. The gap between initial contact and follow-up is often just 24–48 hours—plenty of time for a competitor to swoop in.

**Automation is the fix**

With n8n, you can build automated workflows that trigger instant, personalised follow-ups. For example:
- When a candidate fills in a form, n8n sends a tailored email within 60 seconds.
- If no response after 24 hours, it sends a gentle nudge via SMS or WhatsApp.
- It updates your CRM automatically, so no data entry delays slow you down.

These workflows run 24/7 on a low-code platform, meaning no developer required. For a UK SMB agency, the cost of implementing n8n is a fraction of the revenue saved—often recouped after preventing just one or two placement failures.

**Calculate your agency's risk**

Take your average placement fee. Multiply by the number of placements you lose each month due to slow follow-up. Add in recruiter time and lost referral value. The total likely exceeds £100,000 annually. Automating your follow-up isn’t a nice-to-have—it’s a financial necessity.
