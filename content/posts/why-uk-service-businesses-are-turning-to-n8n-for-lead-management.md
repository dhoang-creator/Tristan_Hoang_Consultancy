## What’s Driving the Shift?

UK service businesses—from electricians to marketing agencies—are drowning in manual lead management. Spreadsheets, missed follow-ups, and time wasted copying data between tools cost both money and opportunities. That’s why many are turning to **n8n**, an open-source automation platform that connects your apps without coding.

## Why n8n Works for Lead Management

### 1. Capture Leads Automatically

n8n pulls leads from anywhere: website forms (e.g., via Webhook), email inquiries, social media messages, or even chatbot conversations. One workflow can parse and clean the data, then route it to your CRM or spreadsheet.

### 2. Qualify with Rules

Set conditions to score leads based on service type, budget, or location. For example, a software consultancy might auto‑assign high‑budget leads to senior sales, while routing smaller enquiries to an automated chatbot flow.

### 3. Send Personalised Follow-Ups

n8n integrates with email tools (Mailgun, SendGrid), SMS (Twilio), and messaging apps (Slack, Teams). Create sequences: send a confirmation email immediately, a case study two days later, and a booking link after one week—all triggered by lead stage.

### 4. Sync Everywhere

Update your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive), Google Sheets, and accounting software in real time. No more duplicate data entry or stale lists.

### 5. Save Thousands on Software Licences

Unlike Zapier or Make, n8n runs on your own infrastructure (or cheap cloud VPS). No per‑workflow pricing. For a UK SMB with 20+ automation workflows, that’s a saving of £200–£500 per month.
