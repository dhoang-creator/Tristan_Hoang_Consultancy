You pride yourself on client service, but let’s be honest: emails slip through the cracks. A query arrives Monday afternoon, you mean to reply Tuesday, and by Thursday the client has sent a follow‑up – or worse, taken their business elsewhere. 

**What does that silence really cost your firm each month?**

### 1. Lost Revenue & Missed Opportunities
Each unanswered email is a potential sale, upsell, or contract renewal. According to a UK‑specific study, 68% of customers cite poor communication as the main reason for leaving a provider. If you lose just one client per month due to slow responses, that’s £500–£2,000 in lost recurring revenue – every single month.

### 2. Damaged Reputation & Trust
In the age of instant messaging, a 48‑hour reply is considered slow. Clients talk. A single negative review or a tweet about “still waiting” can cost you three to five new leads. For a small business, that damage compounds quickly.

### 3. Wasted Employee Time
Your team spends hours each week manually sorting, flagging, and re‑reading emails. Multiply that by an average hourly rate of £25–£35, and you’re burning £200+ per employee per week on administrative overhead. Time that could be spent on billable work or relationship‑building.

### 4. The Hidden Compliance Risk
In regulated UK industries (finance, legal, health), failing to respond to client queries within agreed SLAs can trigger fines or contractual penalties. A single compliance breach can wipe out a month’s profit.
