# Why UK SMBs Are Switching to Self-Hosted Automation with n8n

UK small and medium businesses (SMBs) are increasingly turning to self-hosted automation platforms – and n8n is leading the charge. Here's why.

## 1. GDPR and Data Sovereignty

With Brexit and the UK's own data protection laws, keeping customer data within British borders is more critical than ever. Self-hosted n8n runs on your own servers, so you control exactly where data is processed and stored. No third-party cloud provider accessing your sensitive information.

## 2. Cost Predictability

Cloud automation tools often charge per workflow or per operation, leading to unpredictable bills as your automation grows. With n8n self-hosted, you pay once for the infrastructure (your own VPS or on-premises server) – no per-task fees, no surprise overage charges. For UK SMBs watching margins, that's a game-changer.

## 3. Complete Customisation

n8n's open-source nature means you can tailor every workflow to your exact business processes. Integrate with local UK services like Xero, Sage, or Royal Mail APIs without worrying about platform limitations. Self-hosting gives you the freedom to build what you need.

## 4. Performance and Latency

For UK SMBs with offices in Manchester, London, or Edinburgh, having your automation run locally means faster response times. No waiting for data to travel to a US-based cloud; your workflows execute in milliseconds on your own infrastructure.

## 5. Security and Compliance

Many UK SMBs handle financial data, medical records, or legal documents. Self-hosting n8n allows you to implement your own encryption, access controls, and audit logs, aligning with ISO 27001 or other compliance frameworks without relying on a vendor's promises.

## 6. Community and Support

The n8n community is active and supportive, with many UK-based developers contributing to the platform. You'll find templates for British VAT calculations, HMRC integrations, and more – all ready to deploy on your own server.

## Getting Started

Migrating to self-hosted n8n is straightforward. Choose a UK-based cloud provider for hosting, or run it on your own hardware. The n8n documentation walks you through installation, and our team can help with setup and custom workflows.

Don't let your automation be limited by cloud pricing or data residency fears. Take control with n8n, self-hosted.
